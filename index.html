About MediCompare

MediCompare is designed to make medicine-related price comparison and information easier to understand while keeping medication decisions with qualified healthcare professionals.

This MVP runs on a small, clearly labelled demo dataset. It makes no claim that any two listings are medically interchangeable, and it does not source live pricing.

Why MediCompare

  • Simple comparison. One search, one sorted table.
  • Clear information. Plain language, no clinical jargon.
  • AI-assisted explanations. Ask what a result actually means.
  • User-focused design. Responsive and keyboard accessible.
  • Safety-first approach. No diagnosis, no prescribing, no pressure.

How it works

  1. Enter medicine

    Type the medicine name and the strength printed on the pack.

  2. Compare information

    MediCompare lists every comparison option in its demo dataset.

  3. Understand price differences

    Each option is measured against a reference listing and sorted by price.

  4. Ask the AI assistant

    Get a plain-language explanation, and questions to take to a pharmacist.
